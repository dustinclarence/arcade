Paper Plane Dash

Tap, click or press Space to fly up. Let go to glide down.

How to play

  1. Press Take off.
  2. Tap, click or press Space to lift the plane.
  3. Gravity pulls it down between taps.
  4. Fly through the gaps between the pillars for 1 point each.
  5. Touching a pillar or the ground ends the flight.
