Neon Getaway

Drive with the arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, use the buttons below.

How to play

  1. Press Start the engine.
  2. Drive with the arrow keys or W A S D (phone: the arrow buttons).
  3. Follow the yellow arrow to the next cash bag 💰.
  4. Every bag raises your wanted level, and more police cars chase you.
  5. If a police car catches you, you lose a life. Survive 2 minutes and bank as much cash as you can!
