Four in a Row

Drop your discs into the grid. The first to line up four of the same colour wins.

Your turn. Choose a column.

How to play

  1. Red always goes first. Choose under Game options whether you play Red (first) or Yellow (second).
  2. Click any column to drop a disc. It falls to the lowest empty space.
  3. Take turns with the computer (or a friend).
  4. Connect four discs across, down or diagonally to win.
  5. If the board fills up with no winner, it is a draw.
