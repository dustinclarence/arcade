Pixel Snake

Use the arrow keys, swipe, or the buttons below the game.

How to play

  1. Press Start.
  2. Steer the snake with the arrow keys (or swipe / tap the arrows on a phone).
  3. Eat the red fruit to grow and score points.
  4. Avoid the walls and your own tail.
  5. Press P or Space to pause.
