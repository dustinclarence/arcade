Upfern

Pip bounces by itself. Steer left and right to land on the leaves and climb.

How to play

  1. Press Start climbing. Pip bounces automatically when it lands on a leaf.
  2. Steer left and right. Going off one side of the screen brings you back on the other side.
  3. Green leaves are solid. Blue leaves slide sideways. Brown leaves crumble under you. A spring launches you extra high.
  4. Watch out for thornbugs. Touching one ends the climb, but landing on top of one squashes it for a bonus bounce.
  5. Fall off the bottom of the screen and the climb is over. It gets harder the higher you go.
