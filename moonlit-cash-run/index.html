Moonlit Cash Run

The camera follows behind your car: ↑ drives forward, ← → turn. On a phone, use the buttons below.

How to play

  1. Press Start the engine.
  2. The camera follows behind your car, so ↑ always drives forward and ← → turn the car.
  3. Follow the yellow arrow and the minimap to the next cash bag 💰. The top-left box shows how far it is.
  4. Every bag raises your wanted level, and more police cars chase you through the big city.
  5. If a police car catches you, you lose a life. Bank as much cash as you can in 2½ minutes!
