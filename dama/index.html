Dama Filipino Checkers

The Filipino version of checkers on an 8×8 board. This version uses popular modern rules: pieces capture forwards and backwards, kings fly across the board, and you must always make the biggest capture.

Your turn. Pick a red piece.

How to play

  1. You play Red and move first (in this version).
  2. Click one of your pieces. The green dots show where it can go.
  3. Pieces move one square diagonally forward.
  4. Capture by jumping over an opponent's piece. Pieces can capture forwards and backwards, and can keep jumping.
  5. If you can capture, you must, and you must choose the move that captures the most pieces.
  6. A piece that ends its move on the far side becomes a Dama (★). It is a flying king: it moves any distance along a diagonal.
  7. Capture all pieces, or leave your opponent with no moves, to win.
