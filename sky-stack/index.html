Sky Stack

Tap, click or press Space to drop each floor.

How to play

  1. Press Start building.
  2. Watch the floor swing on the crane.
  3. Tap, click or press Space to drop it on the tower.
  4. Land it close to the centre for a Perfect bonus.
  5. The tower sways, more as it grows taller, so time your drop with the top of the tower.
  6. Off-centre floors make it lean. If too much weight hangs over an edge, the floors above topple.
  7. Miss or topple 3 times and the game ends.
