XOX Tic-Tac-Toe

The classic three-in-a-row game. Can you beat the computer, or at least force a draw?

Your turn. You are X.

How to play

  1. You are X and go first.
  2. Click an empty square to place your mark.
  3. Get three in a row across, down or diagonally to win.
  4. Try Unbeatable mode: the best you can do is a draw!
  5. Press "Next round" to play again. The score is kept.
