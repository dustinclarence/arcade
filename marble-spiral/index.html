Marble Spiral

Aim with your mouse or finger, click or tap to shoot.

How to play

  1. Press Start.
  2. Aim the shooter in the centre with your mouse or finger.
  3. Click or tap to shoot the marble.
  4. Make a group of 3 or more of the same colour to pop them.
  5. Press Space or right-click to swap to the next marble.
  6. Clear the whole chain before it reaches the centre!
